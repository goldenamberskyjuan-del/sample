Logistics & Settlement

Delivery & Payment Details

Delivery & Pickup Options:

Sample delivery coverage: Standard nationwide courier shipping via LBC/J&T and same-day on-demand transport (Grab/Lalamove) within Metro Manila and nearby provinces for rush items. Warehouse pickup is also arranged for confirmed quotation orders.

Payment Terms:

Sample payment options: Bank transfer (BDO, BPI, Metrobank), GCash corporate account, or Check payment upon receipt for pre-approved institutional and clinic accounts. (Note: Demonstration setup only; no live financial checkout).

Sample Details Notice

The logistics routes, freight couriers, and bank accounts presented above represent typical commercial terms and await final confirmation by your business.

These statements are editable anytime via the Admin Dashboard.
